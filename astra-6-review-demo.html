THE MODEL, IN CONTEXT

Capability is the start.
The page is the test.

GPT-6 Astra is an OpenAI model for complex reasoning, coding, computer use, research, and document creation. OpenAI documents its capabilities in the official model reference.

For a website project, turn that broad capability into a specific brief: who the page serves, what it should explain, and what the visitor should do next. Then review the actual page at desktop and phone sizes.

Our editorial approach: assess the result, the revisions, and the evidence behind the claims.

A WORKFLOW YOU CAN REUSE

From the first prompt
to the final click.

Use this sequence when building a business website with an AI coding assistant. Keep the customer’s next decision in view throughout.

  1. 01

    Describe the job of the page.

    State your audience, offer, visual references, and primary action. Supply the real business name, product details, and assets you want to use.

    ↗
  2. 02

    Build around the evidence.

    Place useful detail next to the claim it supports. Customer reviews can sit near a booking, contact, or product decision when they concern the business making that offer.

    ＋
  3. 03

    Add the real widget embed.

    Create your Google Reviews widget, select the correct business source, and use its generated installation code. Follow the Widget House integration workflow.

    ⌘
  4. 04

    Review the published experience.

    Open the public URL in a fresh browser session. Check mobile reading, source attribution, review links, and the next action a visitor should take.

    ✓
